Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Power of Education UG ("we", "us", "our") runs this website to sell notes and solved PYQs to students. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled. By using this site you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details — name, email address, and college/course you provide when you register or log in.
  • Order details — which notes or PYQ packs you purchase, and payment status (we do not store your card, UPI PIN, or bank details — payments are processed by our payment partner).
  • Usage data — pages you visit and searches you make on this site, used only to improve the catalog and folder structure.
  • Support messages — anything you send us via WhatsApp or email for support.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your student account and dashboard.
  • To deliver the notes/PYQs you've purchased and show your purchase history.
  • To respond to support requests and refund claims.
  • To send you order confirmations and, if you opt in, occasional updates about new notes or PYQs.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Payments

Payments are handled through third-party payment gateways (UPI, card, netbanking). We only receive confirmation of payment success/failure — your full card or bank details never pass through or get stored on our servers.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use basic browser storage to keep you logged in and remember your cart/session on this device. This is used only to run the site, not for third-party advertising or tracking.

5. Data Retention

We keep your account and order data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to resolve support/refund requests. You can request deletion of your account at any time.

6. Your Rights

  • You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to correct inaccurate details on your profile.
  • You can request that we delete your account and associated data, subject to any records we're legally required to keep (e.g. payment records).

7. Refunds

As stated on our catalog page, if purchased notes don't meet your expectations, message us on WhatsApp for a refund review — see our Money Back Guarantee.

8. Children's Privacy

This site is intended for college/university students. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy as the site grows. Material changes will be reflected with a new "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us on WhatsApp at @powerofeducationug, 10AM–7PM.
