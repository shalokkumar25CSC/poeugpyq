Who We Are

About Power of Education UG

Power of Education UG started as a simple idea inside MSU Saharanpur: exam-ready notes shouldn't be scattered across ten different WhatsApp groups. We collect, write, and organise notes and solved previous-year question papers (PYQs) for B.A., M.A., and other UG/PG courses, all built around the NEP 2020 syllabus.

Every subject on this site is put together by someone who has actually studied or taught that paper at MSU — not copied from random PDFs floating around the internet. That's why you'll see real names like Shalok Kumar, Afsha, Shivam Kumar, and Sheetal credited on the notes they've written.

Our PYQ Folders section is organised exactly like a library drawer — Course → Subject → Semester → Paper — so you can find last year's question paper for your exact paper in a few clicks, the same way you'd browse a shared drive.
